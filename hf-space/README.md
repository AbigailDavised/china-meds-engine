# China Personal Medication Customs & Supply Checker

Interactive client-side border compliance evaluator powered by [china-meds-engine](https://www.npmjs.com/package/china-meds-engine) and maintained by [ChinaMedsCheck.com](https://chinamedscheck.com/calculator).

- **38 Evaluated Rx & OTC Substances**: Covers prohibited stimulants/cannabinoids, Class I/II psychotropics, biologics/GLP-1s, and common OTC remedies.
- **Red vs. Green Channel Supply Calculator**: Evaluates carried days of supply against GACC Order No. 43 & State Council Decree No. 442 thresholds.
- **Bilingual Chinese Pharmacy Show-Card**: Displays standardized Chinese generic names (`通用名`), Pinyin, and local OTC brand equivalents.

*Educational regulatory reference only; does not constitute clinical medical advice.*
